🐢 Where should your sticker packs live?

The packs folder is one folder on your computer where this app keeps all of your sticker packs together. Every pack you make becomes its own subfolder inside it.

This is not the folder that holds one pack's images. Please don't pick a folder full of pictures — instead pick, or create, a fresh empty folder (for example on your Desktop or in your home folder). The app will make the pack subfolders inside it for you.
