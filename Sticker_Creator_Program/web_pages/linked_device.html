🔗 Linked device

Signal account is linked.

To remove this device, open Signal on your phone: Settings → Linked Devices, , and unlink it there — this app cannot do that step for you.

Deleting local data clears this app's own signal-cli state. It does not touch your Signal account, your messages, or the phone-side link — do that first, above.

Enter the phone number of the Signal account you want to publish sticker packs from, and a name for this device — that name is what Signal lists under Linked Devices on your phone. Then scan the QR code with the Signal app on that account's phone.

In Signal on your phone: Settings → Linked Devices → Link New Device, then scan this code.

Testing something and don't want to link an account yet? You can continue without one — publishing a pack will still require linking later.
